About

Engineer, builder, and a believer in improving life for all humanity.

Aerospace and Manufacturing engineer at Anduril Industries, where I work across design, systems integration, and manufacturing on autonomous defense systems. Before Anduril I was a manufacturing engineer at Lockheed Martin's Skunk Works, supporting advanced development of unmanned jet aircraft.

I have a very nontraditional career. I started at community college, transferred to Cal Poly Pomona, and graduated magna cum laude in Aerospace Engineering with a minor in Astronomy. Along the way I interned at Aerojet Rocketdyne, NASA Armstrong, and NASA Stennis, and published aerospace research with the AIAA.

Today I'm focused on moving deeper into design and systems engineering, with a long-term goal of a PhD and technical leadership.

Design and Research.

AIAA Publication · 2nd Nationally

MAVERICK — Dual Mars Ascent Vehicle

Conceptual design and mission profile for a dual Mars Ascent Vehicle, developed for an undergraduate space design competition where it placed 2nd in the nation. The work covered vehicle configuration, staging, and mission architecture for returning samples off the Martian surface, and was published by the American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics. Faculty advisor: Dr. Navid Nakhjiri.

Read the paper (DOI: 10.2514/6.2023-4779) ↗
Computational Fluid Dynamics · Python

Panel Method Fluid Dynamics

A from-scratch panel-method solver in Python modeling potential flow around a cylinder, with and without rotation — capturing lift generation via the Magnus effect. Built with full support for varying panel resolution to study convergence, with the accompanying technical paper written in LaTeX.
